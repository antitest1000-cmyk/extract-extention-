How to Install OmniExtract (No Web Store Fee)

Because OmniExtract is 100% free and open-source, you don't need to download it through the official store or pay any fees. Follow this step-by-step guide to load the extension in Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, or Opera.

1

Download & Extract the Zip Package

Takes 5 seconds

Click the download link below to save OmniExtract-v1.0.zip to your computer. Right-click the downloaded zip file and select "Extract All..." (or use 7-Zip/Unzip) to extract the files into a dedicated folder (such as Downloads/omniextract or Documents/Extensions/omniextract).

Download OmniExtract-v1.0.zip (80 KB) ↓

Note: Keep this extracted folder on your drive—do not delete it after installing, as your browser loads extension files directly from it.

2

Open Browser Extensions Manager

Takes 3 seconds

Open a new tab in your Chromium browser and paste the appropriate address into your URL address bar:

  • Google Chrome: chrome://extensions
  • Brave Browser: brave://extensions
  • Microsoft Edge: edge://extensions
  • Opera: opera://extensions
3

Turn On "Developer Mode"

Takes 2 seconds

In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, toggle the switch labeled "Developer mode" to the ON position. When enabled, three new action buttons will appear on the top toolbar: "Load unpacked", "Pack extension", and "Update".

4

Click "Load Unpacked" & Select Folder

Takes 5 seconds

Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top-left corner. In the file picker dialog, navigate to the folder where you extracted OmniExtract (the directory containing manifest.json) and click "Select Folder".

Success! OmniExtract will immediately appear in your browser's extension list with a blue badge.

5

Pin OmniExtract for Instant 1-Click Access

Pro Tip

Click the puzzle piece icon (Extensions menu) in the top-right corner of your browser toolbar, find OmniExtract in the list, and click the Pin icon. Now the globe icon will remain permanently visible on your browser header for one-click harvesting anytime!

Frequently Asked Installation Questions

Q: Will Chrome show a warning about developer mode?

Google Chromium may occasionally show a harmless reminder that unpacked extensions are installed. You can simply click outside or dismiss the prompt. Since OmniExtract is 100% open source, you can inspect every line of source code yourself.

Q: How do I update to newer versions?

When a new release is posted, simply download the new zip file and replace the files inside your existing extracted folder. Then go to chrome://extensions and click the circular Reload arrow on the OmniExtract card.

Q: Why does the popup say "Please reload webpage (F5)"?

If you installed OmniExtract while existing web tabs were already open, refresh the target tab once with F5 so your browser injects the extension's content script into the page.
